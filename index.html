Your Guide to the Election Process

Tap a stage to explore what happens, when it happens, and how you can participate.

1

Registration & Preparation

Months before Election

Before you can vote, you must register! Deadlines and rules vary by state.

  • Check your voter registration status.
  • Update your address if you've moved.
  • Research what's on your local ballot.
2

Primaries & Caucuses

Spring / Summer

Political parties choose their candidates for the general election during this phase.

  • Primary: Vote by secret ballot for your preferred candidate.
  • Caucus: A local gathering where voters decide candidates together.
3

General Election Day

Early November

The big day! Millions head to the polls or mail in their ballots.

  • Bring required ID (if applicable in your state).
  • Find your polling place early or mail your ballot on time.
  • Every vote counts towards the final result!
4

Results & Transition

Late Nov - January

Votes are certified and the transition of power begins.

  • Votes are tallied and officially certified by each state.
  • The Electoral College meets (for Presidential elections).
  • Inauguration Day marks the official start of the new term.
